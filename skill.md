# Workshop Planning

## Purpose
Estimate workshop cost and verify resources.

## Inputs
participants, days, daily_cost_per_person, budget, required_items

## Workflow
1. calculate_budget
2. check_resources
3. summarise both results in clear language

## Constraints
Currency is strictly INR. Do not invent inventory. One call per step.

## Error handling
On a tool error, report it. Do not retry blindly.
